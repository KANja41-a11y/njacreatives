✦ my creative journey

Growing through little things.

01

Curious

It started with tiny ideas, random sketches, and a lot of “what if?” moments.

Every doodle in the margin, every photo of morning light, every “maybe I can make this” — they all became the seed of something larger.
02

Creating

Ideas became designs, stories, photos, websites, and experiments that felt like me.

From soft product photos to digital playgrounds, each piece is a small experiment in turning imagination into something you can hold or click.
03

Becoming

Still learning, still trying, still turning imagination into something real.

This playground is part of that becoming — a place to keep creating, sharing, and growing with soft curiosity and a little sparkle.
